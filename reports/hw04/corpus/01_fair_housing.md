# Federal Fair Housing Act

The federal Fair Housing Act protects people from housing discrimination based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, and disability. Housing providers may not refuse to rent or sell, refuse to negotiate, make housing unavailable, impose different rental terms, or provide different housing services because of a protected class. Familial status includes households with children under 18, pregnant people, and people securing custody of children.

For disability-related needs, a housing provider may need to allow reasonable accommodations to rules or services and reasonable modifications to a dwelling when they are necessary for equal use and enjoyment of the housing.
