# Maintenance Requests and Records

Tenants should report maintenance concerns through the communication method identified in the lease or property-management instructions. A useful request includes the unit identifier, a description of the problem, when it began, and whether there is an urgent safety concern. Tenants should keep copies of requests, photographs, and responses.

Landlords should maintain a record of requests and responses, coordinate access when an inspection or repair is needed, and communicate expected timing. Maintenance records help both parties understand what was reported and what action was taken.
