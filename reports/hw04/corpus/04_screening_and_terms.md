# Rental Screening and Consistent Terms

Rental screening criteria should be applied consistently to applicants. A landlord may use lawful criteria such as income, rental history, credit history, and references, but the same stated criteria should be applied without treating applicants differently because of a protected class. Rental terms, services, and availability decisions should also be consistent with fair-housing requirements.

A landlord should document the criteria used, avoid questions that reveal protected information unnecessarily, and communicate application decisions consistently. Screening policies do not override federal or state fair-housing protections.
