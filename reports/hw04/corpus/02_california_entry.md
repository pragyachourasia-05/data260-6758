# California Rental Entry Notice

California landlords generally provide reasonable written notice before entering a rental unit. Twenty-four hours is commonly treated as reasonable notice for ordinary entry. Notice should identify the approximate date, time, and purpose of the entry when required by the applicable rule.

Notice is not required in every situation. Important exceptions can include an emergency, a court order, abandonment or surrender of the unit, or permission given by the tenant at the time of entry. The exact application depends on the reason for entry and the governing law.
